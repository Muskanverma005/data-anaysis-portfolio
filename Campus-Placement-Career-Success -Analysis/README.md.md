\# 👩‍💼 **Campus Placement and Career Success Analysis**



\## 📌 **Objective**

Analyze student placement data to identify the key factors influencing placement outcomes and salary packages. The project focuses on academic performance, internships, skills, and other career-related factors.



\## 📂 **Dataset**

A  dataset  of  20,000  student  records  containing :

\-academic

\-technical

\- professional

\-demographic

\-placement-related information.



\## 🛠 **Tools \& Techniques Used**

\-Microsoft  Excel

\- Pivot  Tables

\- Charts

\-Data Analysis \& Dashboard Visualization

\-Slicers



\## 📈 **Key Insights**

\-Higher  CGPA  and  internship  experience  are  associated  with  higher  average  salaries.

\-The  analysis  also  highlights  salary  trends  across  specializations ,  college tiers ,  skills ,  and  other  career - readiness  factors.



\## 📊 **Dashboard Features**

An  interactive  Excel  dashboard  presenting  KPIs ,  salary  trends ,  specialization  analysis ,  CGPA  vs  salary ,  internship analysis ,  demographics ,  and  skill-based  insights  through  charts  and  visualizations.

\## 🚀 **Conclusion**

The  project  demonstrates  how  Excel  can  transform  raw  placement  data  into  meaningful  business  insights .

It  provides  a  data-driven  view  of  student  career  readiness  and  placement  outcomes.
